Questions

Pricing FAQ

How does the 90-day free trial work?

Pick Basic, Growth or Premium and start your trial. A card is required, but you are not charged during the first 90 days. Cancel in the Stripe customer portal before day 90 to pay nothing. After the trial your plan renews at the price you chose.

What is the optional success fee?

If you turn on Managed bookings, SonrisaConnect coordinates a booking for a patient and charges 7% of the treatment value (5% on Premium) only after the patient completes treatment at your clinic. There is no fee for inquiries, no-shows or your existing patients. You can leave it off and use the listing only.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time from your Stripe billing portal. Cancelling stops the next renewal and your listing stays live until the end of the period you paid for.

How much do I save with annual billing?

Annual billing is 20% cheaper than paying monthly: $276 a year for Basic, $660 for Growth and $1,428 for Premium.

Do you guarantee patients?

No. SonrisaConnect is a marketplace that gives your clinic a trusted profile, tools and visibility with US patients. We cannot guarantee a number of inquiries or bookings, and we do not sell patient leads.

How is my clinic verified?

You share your license or RETHUS number and supporting documents in the onboarding form. We review them before showing the Verified badge. Practices that cannot be verified are not given the badge.

What currency and payment methods?

Prices are in US dollars. Subscriptions are paid by card through Stripe. Patients pay your clinic directly for treatment, and the clinic sets its own prices.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No setup fee and no long-term contract. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time, and annual plans cover one year of service.
